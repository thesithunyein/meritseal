    zkVerify
    Every receipt carries a real proof
    Each ZK receipt contains a Groth16 proof that a Poseidon commitment opens to an amount within €5,000–€50,000. Anyone can verify it in the browser against the published key, and the proof is also submitted to zkVerify through Kurier so the verification is recorded on-chain.
    The key manifest lists the circuit, the verification-key hash and how the key was generated.

    FAQ
    

    

    What stays private?
    The eligibility check runs in your browser. The receipt excludes the requested amount and applicant details.
    

    What does a receipt show?
    It records the policy version, outcome, and time. Its checksum lets you compare it against the original receipt.
    

    Is this a zero-knowledge proof?
    The current receipt uses a SHA-256 checksum. It does not prove the decision or identify the issuer.
    

    Which policy does the check use?
    It uses NLnet’s published first-grant amount range. This check covers one criterion. NLnet decides eligibility and awards.
