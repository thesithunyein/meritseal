How it works

Three checks, one private report

Each part does one job, and your budget never travels.

🤖

The AI reads, without the numbers

Before your text is sent, every amount is replaced with [AMOUNT]. The AI (Google Gemini) checks seven of NLnet’s published requirements and quotes the part of your text it relied on.

🔒

Your budget is proven, not shown

Your browser creates a zero-knowledge proof: a short piece of math that shows your budget is between €5,000 and €50,000 without revealing the number. A budget outside the range cannot produce a proof.

🌐

Anyone can check it

The proof is verified on zkVerify, a public network built for checking proofs, so a funder or auditor can confirm it later. You do not need a wallet, an account or any crypto.

Technical details

The budget proof is a Groth16 proof (snarkjs, circom) that a Poseidon commitment opens to a whole-euro amount between 5,000 and 50,000. The verification key and setup are published at /zk/manifest.json. Proofs are submitted to zkVerify Volta testnet through the Kurier relayer. The agent returns APPROVE only when all seven rules pass and the proof verifies. Source: github.com/thesithunyein/meritseal.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need a crypto wallet or tokens?

No. MeritSeal runs in your browser. The public proof record is paid for by the service, and you never sign anything.

Is my budget sent anywhere?

No. Your budget stays on your device. Only the proof is sent, and the proof does not contain the number. Amounts inside your application text are removed before the text leaves your browser. You can see exactly what the AI received under “What the AI saw” in your report.

Is this official NLnet?

No. MeritSeal is an independent tool that checks your text against rules NLnet publishes on nlnet.nl/propose. NLnet makes its own decisions. Treat the report as a pre-check.

Can the AI be wrong?

Yes. Every verdict includes the quote it relied on, so you can judge it yourself. The budget check is different: it is decided by math, not by the AI.

What is the “secret file”?

When your budget is proven, your browser can save a small file with the exact amount and a random number. If a funder later asks you to confirm the real amount, that file proves it matches your proof. Keep it private.

Who sees my application text?

The text, with amounts removed, is sent to Google Gemini for analysis and is not stored by MeritSeal. On Google’s free tier, Google may use prompts to improve its models, so avoid pasting anything you consider confidential beyond the proposal itself.
